I always thought AI had a steep barrier to entry. Once I actually started, it turned out you can learn it in layers.

## Step 1: just use it

Call an LLM API and get a small task working (summarizing, classifying). Positive feedback is what keeps you going.

## Step 2: understand what's happening

Get clear on prompts, tokens, and context windows — why the model answers the way it does.

## Step 3: then the theory

When you have the energy, look into transformers and training. No rush; fill it in as you go.

> Get it running first, dig deeper later. Starting with theory is a fast way to quit.
