Say you change a button color and conversion goes from 10% to 11%. Is that 1% a real improvement, or just luck? A/B testing answers exactly that.

## The core idea

Split users randomly into two groups. Group A sees the old version, group B the new one, everything else equal. Any difference comes only from that change.

## Don't get fooled by small samples

One extra conversion out of 100 people looks like a 1% lift, but it could easily be noise. Too small a sample and no conclusion holds.

## Significance

Statistical significance just means: the odds of this difference showing up "by chance" are low enough that we dare call it real.

> Data analysis isn't about producing a number — it's about judging whether the number can be trusted.
